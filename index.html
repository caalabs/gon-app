How it works

Three steps. Nothing hidden.

Progress shows where the work happens, so you always know what Gon is doing and why.

  1. 1

    Scan

    Every section looks at once and reports on its own: looking, done, or nothing found. Sizes roll in as they're measured.

  2. 2

    Review

    Safe items are ticked for you. Anything that might hold your data waits for your call, and a cache stays put while its app is open.

  3. 3

    Clean

    Items leave one by one, into the Trash, where you can put them back. A short result tells you what moved and where it came from.

Six tabs

One calm window.

Each part of Gon has its own color, so you always know where you are. Everything runs on your Mac.

Clean

Caches, logs, build leftovers and old installers, each with what it is and what happens if it goes.

AI Tools

Caches and old builds of AI coding tools, chats whose project is gone, worktrees you finished with.

Apps

Uninstall an app with everything it left behind, tidy what starts at login, update Homebrew apps.

Analyze

A map of the whole disk with the largest files beside it. Shared and cloned files count once.

Optimize

Maintenance and quick fixes, with one password prompt and a result for every task.

Status

Live CPU, memory, disk, network and battery, every process, and a health check.

In your menu bar

Gon keeps you company.

Gon hops in the menu bar, faster as your Mac gets busier. Prefer something quieter? Pick bubbles, pulse bars, a heartbeat or a fan, with CPU, memory or network beside it.

Safety

Nothing leaves without your review.

The Trash by default

What Gon removes goes to the Trash, where you can put it back. Deleting for good always asks first.

Hands off what's in use

A cache stays while the app or tool that writes it runs, and a file an app has open is skipped.

Your places stay yours

Keychains, Mail, Messages and your documents are never offered. Add any folder to the protected list.

A record of everything

Every removed or skipped item is written to a log you can open from Settings.

Give your Mac some room.
