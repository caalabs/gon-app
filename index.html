Analyze

See where the space really went.

A treemap of every folder, sized as it is measured, with the largest files beside it. Open any block to go deeper; move what you don't need to the Trash from right there.

  • Numbers that add up. Hard-linked files and APFS clones count once, so folders never add up to more than the disk. Next update
  • Measured as it goes. Sizes stream in folder by folder; what can't be read yet shows as “at least”, never as a guess.
  • Opens where you left off. The last map shows at once and is measured again behind it. Next update
  • “Why is my disk full?” One question, a few plain lines, and a button to the place that frees the space. Next update
Why is my disk full?
Most of the space is in your home folder: Docker’s disk image and old project build folders.
  • Docker keeps 61 GB of images. Remove unused ones in Docker's settings.
  • Old build folders in your projects take 12.6 GB and come back on the next build.Open Clean
  • About 60 GB is macOS itself and snapshots, which macOS manages.

Apps

Gone means gone.

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves its settings, caches and helpers behind. Gon finds them, shows how it knows, and removes them with the app.

  • Evidence for every file. Matched by bundle ID, developer team, installer receipt or the app's helpers — never by a guess alone.
  • Leftovers of apps you already deleted, found on their own and grouped by app.
  • One password, then the Trash. Files only an administrator can move go in one prompt — into your Trash, so you can put them back.
  • Startup items and updates in the same place, including apps from Homebrew and the App Store.

Safety

Built to be trusted
with your files.

A cleaner is only as good as what it refuses to touch. Every removal in Gon goes through one gate.

You review first

Nothing moves until you press Move to Trash. Deep links and automation can scan, never remove.

Trash by default

Even files that need an administrator go to your Trash, so a change can always be undone.

Protected places

System folders, Keychains, Mail, Messages, SSH keys, sign-in files and your documents are never touched.

Checked at the last moment

If a file was swapped or replaced after the scan, Gon leaves it alone and tells you. Next update

Every file on record

A log of each operation — and, next, of every file: when it went, how, and how big it was.

Caches macOS needs

Sync, security and sign-in caches stay put — and next, fonts, Spotlight and the system's own model caches too. Next update
