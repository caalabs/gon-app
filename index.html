Gon's rules.

  • The Trash, never the void. Deleting for good always asks first.
  • Hands off what's in use. A cache stays while its app runs; open files are skipped.
  • Your places stay yours. Keychains, Mail, Messages and documents are never offered.
  • A note of everything. Every removed or skipped item goes in a log you can open.
